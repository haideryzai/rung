RUNG Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

RUNG is an online multiplayer Court Piece / Rang card game. This policy explains exactly what the game stores, why it needs it, and how to get rid of it. It covers the RUNG Android app and the game servers behind it.

The short version. You can play without giving us anything: the app creates an anonymous account by itself. We store what a competitive game needs — a name, a rating, match results, friends. We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and there is no real-money gambling. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

3. What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising SDK in the app.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. The app requests only internet and network-state permissions.
  • No hardware identifiers such as IMEI or MAC address.
  • No voice or free-text chat. In-game communication is a fixed set of reactions, which keeps a conversation from being something we have to store or moderate.

4. Why we are allowed to hold it

We process this data to provide the game you asked to play (performing our agreement with you), and to keep it fair and safe for everyone — preventing cheating and abuse — which is our legitimate interest. Where consent is the right basis, such as linking a Google account, you give it by choosing to do so and can withdraw it by deleting your account.

5. Who else sees it

  • Other players see your display name, avatar, country flag, rating and rank, your presence, and results of matches you played together. Your email address is never shown to another player.
  • Google, only if you use Google sign-in, and only as part of that sign-in. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the servers storing this data on our behalf.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and progression data: until you delete your account.
  • Match records: kept as shared history — a match belongs to four players, and a result cannot be removed for one of them without corrupting the other three. After you delete your account those records no longer carry your name or any link to you.
  • Live state (presence, queue, current table): minutes to hours; it expires automatically.
  • Server logs: a limited retention period, then deleted.

7. Your choices

Delete everything

In the app: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. This removes your profile, rating, statistics, friends, achievements, missions, inventory and sign-in details immediately and permanently. Matches you already played remain in the other players’ history, anonymised. It cannot be undone.

Get a copy of your data

Ask us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we will send a machine-readable export of what your account holds.

Correct it

You can change your display name and avatar at any time in the app. For anything else, contact us.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, or to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

  • Sign-in tokens are short-lived, and the long-lived refresh token is stored only as a hash on our side — a copy of our database cannot be replayed against the service.
  • Passwords, where used, are stored hashed with bcrypt, never in plain text.
  • Google sign-in tokens are verified against Google’s published keys before an account is touched.
  • Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.

No service can promise perfect security, but we design so that a single failure does not expose your account.

9. Children

RUNG is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our servers may be located outside your country. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards for that transfer.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
